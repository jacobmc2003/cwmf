Her story, still teaching

Carrie Williams spent her career teaching at a local Catholic school, and the way she showed up for her students left a mark that didn't end when she passed away. Her family and friends started this foundation to keep that spirit going — one euchre tournament at a time.

Every card played and every dollar raised funds the Carrie Williams Memorial Scholarship, supporting children at Catholic schools across the Diocese of Covington. Now in its fifth year, the tournament brings together about 150 players for an evening that's equal parts competitive and heartfelt.
